FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need a desk phone or special hardware?

No. RingJust runs in your web browser. A laptop and a headset are all you need.

Can I keep my existing business number?

Yes. We help you move (port) your current number to RingJust, or you can pick a new local number.

Which countries can I call?

RingJust currently supports calling and texting within the United States and Canada.

Is it only for large teams?

Not at all. RingJust works for a one-person business as well as a team of fifty. You pay per seat, so it grows with you.

Can I use RingJust for text message marketing?

RingJust is built for conversations with people who expect to hear from you. Opt-outs with STOP and HELP are handled automatically.
