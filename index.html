Features

Everything a broker's phone needs to do, and nothing it doesn't

Built by a team that dispatches freight for US brokerages every night, so it is shaped around the calls brokers actually make.

Click to call

Call any carrier, shipper or driver straight from the browser with one click. No desk phone, no softphone install.

Local US numbers

Pick numbers by area code so your calls show up local. Carriers answer numbers they recognise.

Two-way SMS

Text drivers and carriers from the same number you call from, with the full thread kept in one place.

Automatic call logging

Every call and text is saved to the contact with time, duration and outcome, so nobody has to write it up.

Built-in CRM

Carriers, shippers and deals on the same login as your phone. Call history sits next to every account.

Team seats
